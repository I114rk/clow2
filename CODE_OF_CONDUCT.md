# Code of Conduct

CLOW is an open source community for inclusive and respectful collaboration.

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be kind and courteous.
- Respect different opinions and experiences.
- Focus on what is best for the community.
- Do not engage in abusive or exclusionary behavior.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please open an issue or contact the maintainers.
